1. **Minimalist Top Bar**

2. **Hamburger Menu for Mobile**

3. **Sticky Navigation**

### **Typography Systems**
1. **Hierarchical Font Pairing**
   - **Headings**: Futuristic sans-serif (e.g., *Inter*, *Rajdhani*)
   - **Body**: Clean sans-serif (e.g., *Open Sans*, *Lato*)
   - **Accent**: Monospace for code snippets (e.g., *Fira Code*)

2. **Variable Font Weights**
   - Bold (700) for CTAs, medium (500) for headings

3. **Text Effects**
   - Subtle glow or shadow on headings
   - Letter spacing adjustments for premium feel
